02 — THE ORIGIN

THE MAURITANIAN ATLANTIC

A coastline shaped by
cold currents & abundance.

Where the Canary Current meets the Atlantic upwelling, nutrient-rich waters create one of West Africa's most productive marine environments.

From Nouadhibou and the wider Mauritanian Atlantic, we connect this natural abundance with international buyers through dependable sourcing, careful handling, cold-chain logistics and export-ready preparation.
